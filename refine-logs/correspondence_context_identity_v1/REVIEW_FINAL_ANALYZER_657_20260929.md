## B. Score normalization — PASS

AP is the mean precision at actual positive ranks; mAP is the query mean times 100, and CMC is the percentage of queries whose first match falls within ranks 1/5/10 (`tools/train_rgbnt100_signal_oof.py:257`, `:263`, `:265`; `tools/train_msvr310_signal_oof.py:227`, `:233`, `:235`). There is no division by a model's best score, maximum output, or observed prediction mean.
