## B. Mathematics And Executable Metric Path: PASS

The actual V23 adapter formula is stage-specific and modality-specific, with one residual MLP per known modality. The code builds `ModuleList` adapters over `MODALITY_ORDER` (`modeling/trifusion/signal_preserving_v23.py:16` through `modeling/trifusion/signal_preserving_v23.py:20`), initializes down weights with Kaiming and zeroes both biases plus the up weight (`modeling/trifusion/signal_preserving_v23.py:21` through `modeling/trifusion/signal_preserving_v23.py:25`), and returns `sequence[:, modality] + adapter(sequence[:, modality])` (`modeling/trifusion/signal_preserving_v23.py:27` through `modeling/trifusion/signal_preserving_v23.py:31`). It constructs one spectral stage per frozen tail layer and applies that same stage inside each expert loop, so the adapters are shared across CNN/Transformer/Mamba roles but isolated by modality and stage (`modeling/trifusion/signal_preserving_v23.py:37` through `modeling/trifusion/signal_preserving_v23.py:45`, `modeling/trifusion/signal_preserving_v23.py:51` through `modeling/trifusion/signal_preserving_v23.py:61`). The reported count is exact: 9 * ((768*128 + 128) + (128*768 + 768)) = 1,777,536 new parameters.

The training loss is the configured V8 identity/triplet loss: fused ID/triplet, per-branch ID/triplet, and per-residual ID/triplet. V23 combines these coefficients in `loss_terms` (`tools/train_signal_preserving_v23.py:109` through `tools/train_signal_preserving_v23.py:125`), and the config fixes the same weights, margin, and smoothing (`configs/RGBNT201/TriFusion-signal-preserving-v23-spectral-adapter-rtx3090.yml:39` through `configs/RGBNT201/TriFusion-signal-preserving-v23-spectral-adapter-rtx3090.yml:47`). The criterion consumes real batch identity labels, applies cross entropy with label smoothing, and applies batch-hard triplet loss to normalized embeddings (`modeling/trifusion/signal_preserving_v8.py:696` through `modeling/trifusion/signal_preserving_v8.py:742`).
