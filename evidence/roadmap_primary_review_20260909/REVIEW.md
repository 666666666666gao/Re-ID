# ROADMAP primary-source review — conditional follow-up only

Read on 2026-09-09. Paper: [arXiv 2110.01445v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2110.01445v3), sections 3.1–3.2, equations 2–7 and experimental settings. Author implementation pinned to [fed37d75f475f636542b2ccd1ffc0c3918498a57](https://github.com/elias-ramzi/ROADMAP/tree/fed37d75f475f636542b2ccd1ffc0c3918498a57). Six source files retained byte-for-byte with MIT license and source manifest. No author package installed, imported or run; no new training registered.

Paper mechanism (primary-source paraphrase): SupAP keeps the exact positive rank without its gradient, while negative comparisons use a sigmoid with a shifted positive branch and linear tail. ROADMAP combines it with pairwise score calibration, addressing the discrepancy between batch and union rankings. Paper defaults include tau .01, rho 100, calibration thresholds .9/.6, and equal half-weights. Its retrieval evaluations use mAP@R on CUB, SOP and iNaturalist, not TriFusion's camera/scene-filtered ReID protocol. Neither its surrogate bound nor its batch analysis proves unseen-identity ReID improvement. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2110.01445v3)

Verified implementation differences:

- `smooth_rank_ap.py:29–55`: positive differences use Heaviside; negative t=0 takes the sigmoid branch (.5), whereas positive ties use Heaviside(0)=1. A later adaptation must specify ties; do not quote a universal bound without its tie convention.
- `SupAP` default offset is None, producing the formula's continuous tail intercept. `roadmap.yaml:13` instead supplies 1.44 at delta=.05. At tau=.01, the formula intercept is sigmoid(5)+.5 = 1.4933071490757153. This is a real paper/config distinction, not a diagnosed TriFusion bug.
- YAML lists both objective weights as 1.0. Do not assume this is identical in scale to the paper's half-weights or to replacing one unit-weight term among TriFusion's 14 terms; downstream aggregation still needs inspection before reproduction.
- Calibration delegates to `pytorch_metric_learning.ContrastiveLoss`; its dependency's reduction and self/reference behavior were not independently checked here. Square/general AP paths also differ. Do not copy these interfaces into the dynamic history queue without defining the actual candidate mask.
- README line126 discloses changed code/results. Paper, class defaults and named YAML must be identified separately. [Pinned code](https://github.com/elias-ramzi/ROADMAP/blob/fed37d75f475f636542b2ccd1ffc0c3918498a57/roadmap/losses/smooth_rank_ap.py), [configuration](https://github.com/elias-ramzi/ROADMAP/blob/fed37d75f475f636542b2ccd1ffc0c3918498a57/roadmap/config/loss/roadmap.yaml).
